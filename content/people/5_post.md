Smit Sekhadia and I spent the summer of 26 as roommates. Smit came in my life like a baal krishna. He is geniunely very nice, caring, discplined, hard-working and always puts other first. He is all this while singing and dancing his way through life (literally, all the time). He will annoy you with harmless fun and be in his own moj-masti. He even has gopis with him all the time (7 boys from his undergrand, on video calls). These qualities of him is the reason why I call him Lala at times (Lala means baal krishna in Gujarati).

Smit has been me a rock for me, always very supportive throught the internship. Whenever I would feel low, he would cheer me up. He would make a lot of fun plans with me, he would make sure that I am friends with all his friends too. Wherever he would go, he would always invite me. As a roommate too he has been amazing, he has always been super supportive of everything, which tells a lot about him and the way he lives life. He would always put others first, he is selfless and makes sure people around him are happy and winning in life.

Smit is very disciplined. He works pretty hard at his job and makes sure he is hitting the gym regularly. He would push me at the gym too, true to his general life disposition wherein he wants to grow and everyone around him to grow too. Smit lives a simple, spiritual life, without a whole lot of desires and just being happy in the present. We all should be like him a little bit.
